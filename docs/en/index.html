A proper family house

A quiet stone country house with a large garden and a private pool. It's made for bringing a family or a group of friends together: big bedrooms, a big table, and plenty to keep everyone busy.

Downstairs, the fitted kitchen with its utility room, a stone-walled dining room and a living room with a pool table. Upstairs, four large bedrooms. The bedrooms at the back of the house are level with the garden and open straight onto it.

Around the house

The house sits where Corrèze, the Lot and the Dordogne meet. Most of the region's highlights are less than an hour's drive away.

Getting here

By car via the A20 or A89 motorways, Brive exit. By train to Brive-la-Gaillarde station, or by plane to Brive–Vallée de la Dordogne airport, both about 15 minutes away. You'll need a car once you're here.

Good to know

How do I book?

Bookings, payment and cancellation terms are handled by Airbnb. The “Check availability” button takes you straight there, with up-to-date prices for your dates.

When is the house available?

The house is let from mid-May to the end of September.

How does check-in work?

Check-in from 5 pm, on your own with a secure key box, so you can arrive whenever suits you. Check-out by 10 am.

Is the pool safe for children?

Yes. It's private, fenced and locked. It's open from June to September, so it isn't running yet for stays in May.

Where do we park?

In the large courtyard in front of the house, which easily takes several cars.

Is linen provided?

Yes, bed linen and bath towels are included.

Can we hold a party?

The house welcomes families and groups of friends, but parties and events aren't allowed.

Can I work from the house?

Yes: the Wi-Fi runs on fibre, and three bedrooms have a desk.

Where can we shop?

Supermarkets in Brive are about ten minutes away by car.
