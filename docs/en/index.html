The house

A family home, made for sharing

Set in the quiet Corrèze countryside, the house has kept the soul of a family home: sandstone walls, old beams, antique furniture and large bedrooms.

It's a place to come as a group, for long days by the pool, dinners that linger in the garden and evenings around the pool table.

  • Sleeps 12
  • 4 spacious bedrooms
  • 3 bathrooms
  • Private pool, from early May
  • Large grounds, wooded and fenced
  • 10 min from Brive

The elegance of a family home, the joy of a country holiday.

Cosnac · Between Corrèze, Lot and Dordogne

Practical info

Good to know

Booking and availability

Bookings and payment are handled on Airbnb, where availability and prices are kept up to date. The house is available from mid-May to the end of September.

Check-in and check-out

Self check-in from 5 pm with a secure key box. Check-out before 10 am. Parking in the large courtyard.

The pool

Private, fenced and locked. It is open from early May.

The grounds

The grounds are fenced, so children can play outside safely.

Linen and amenities

Bed linen and towels provided. Fully equipped kitchen, washing machine, fibre Wi-Fi, desks in three bedrooms.

Parties and events

The house welcomes families and groups of friends; parties and events are not allowed.
